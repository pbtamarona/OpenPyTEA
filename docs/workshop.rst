Workshop
========

Open-Science Techno-Economic Assessment with OpenPyTEA: From Process Design to Economic Insights
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On November 6, 2026, we hosted the first OpenPyTEA workshop at TU Delft. More than 70 participants joined from many countries and from across industry, academia, and research institutes.

The day ran from process design and TEA fundamentals, through a hands-on OpenPyTEA session and an industry perspective, to a look at multi-agent systems for process design.

Thank you to everyone who joined us in Delft, to our invited speakers Arnoud Higler and Artur Schweidtmann, to fellow organizers Thijs Vlugt and Mahinder Ramdin, and to the TU Delft Process & Product Technology Institute (Pro2Tech) and the TU Delft Institute for Computational Science and Engineering for making it possible.

The workshop materials stay online at `github.com/pbtamarona/openpytea_workshop <https://github.com/pbtamarona/openpytea_workshop>`_. Want to try OpenPyTEA and get involved? Everything is open source on `GitHub <https://github.com/pbtamarona/OpenPyTEA>`_.

Keep an eye out for the next one! 👀
